About me

I build things that are simple, precise, and purposeful.

I'm a multidisciplinary designer and front-end developer based in Paris. I believe the best work sits at the intersection of rigorous craft and quiet restraint — nothing superfluous, everything intentional.

With over five years of experience working with agencies, startups, and independent clients, I bring both strategic thinking and meticulous execution to every project.
